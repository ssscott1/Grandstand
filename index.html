Good questions

New here? Start here.

I've never done CrossFit before. Can I still join?
Absolutely — most of our members started exactly there. Every workout is coached and scaled to your level, and our beginner onboarding teaches you the foundational movements safely before you jump into classes. Your first session is free.
Where are you located, and is there parking?
We're at 92 Parry Street, Perth WA 6000 — right near HBF Park on the edge of the CBD, minutes from East Perth, Highgate, Northbridge, Mount Lawley and Leederville. Street parking is available nearby, and we're an easy walk from Claisebrook and McIver stations.
Can I bring my kids while I train?
Yes — Grandstand has a gated kids crèche area with a child-proof lock, right inside the gym. Kids can relax on the bean bags, play with the toys, draw on the chalkboard or watch Netflix while you train. It's one of our favourite points of difference.
Do you run Hyrox training in Perth?
Yes — every Sunday at 9am we run a dedicated Hyrox session: sled pushes and pulls, ski erg, wall balls, lunges and running intervals. It's the perfect supplement to your CrossFit training through the week, whether you're targeting your first Hyrox Perth event or chasing a PB.
What memberships do you offer?
Unlimited (all classes, open gym and specialty sessions), 3-sessions-per-week and 2-sessions-per-week options — with flexible arrangements for FIFO workers. Casual drop-ins are $30 a session, or visitors can grab an $80 unlimited week pass. See pricing for details.
What should I bring to my first class?
Comfortable training clothes, flat-soled shoes if you have them (any runners are fine to start), a water bottle and a towel. We'll handle the rest — including the encouragement.
