FAQs

PT questions, answered

Who is personal training best for?
Anyone who wants faster progress, has a specific goal (strength, fat loss, a comp, return from injury), trains at odd hours, or simply prefers one-on-one attention before joining classes.
Can I combine PT with classes?
Yes — it's the most effective combo we know. Many members use PT to sharpen a lift or work around an injury while keeping the energy of group classes.
How much does personal training cost?
Rates depend on session length and package size. Tell us your goal via the form or call 0411 371 661 and we'll recommend the right structure and pricing.
