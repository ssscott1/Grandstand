Welcome to the team

This form is used to onboard casual employees to Go Unbroken Pty Ltd trading as Grandstand CrossFit. Please fill it out in full and accurately — it's used to set up your pay, super and tax correctly from day one.

Employee details

Every field marked required is needed to get your first pay run right. If you're not sure about a super or bank detail right now, get in touch with Shez and we'll sort it before your first pay cycle.

  • Have your driver's licence and bank details handy
  • Have your super fund's name, member number and USI ready (check a recent statement or the myGov super page)
  • Know your tax file number — you don't have to provide it, but there are tax consequences if you don't

Your details are submitted securely and go straight to Grandstand's gym management for payroll and super setup — they're not used for anything else, and never shared beyond what's needed to employ you.
