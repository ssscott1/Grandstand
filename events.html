The 2026 Christmas Party

Grab your scrunchies and your best denim-on-denim — Grandstand's Christmas party is going full 90's this year. A DJ, music all night, drinks, food and heaps of fun with the crew who've sweated it out with you all year.

What's on

  • 90's theme — think scrunchies, flannel, bucket hats and neon. Prizes for best dressed.
  • Live DJ spinning all night, from throwback 90's hits to the stuff that gets everyone on the floor.
  • Drinks flowing upstairs at The Elford all evening.
  • Food to keep you going from 6pm to midnight.
  • Heaps of fun with the Grandstand crew — the whole reason we do this.

This one's for Grandstand members — bring your training partners, your favourite coach and your best 90's outfit. Tickets are limited, so get in early.

Good questions

Party FAQs

How do I pay for my tickets?
Once you submit the form, our team adds your tickets straight to your Wodify account, the same place your membership payments go. We'll confirm by text or email once it's done.
Is this open to the public?
The Christmas party is a members' event. If you're not yet a Grandstand member and keen to come along, get in touch first — contact us or book a free trial.
What should I wear?
It's a 90's theme — scrunchies, flannel, denim, neon, bucket hats, the works. There's a prize for best dressed, so make an effort.
Where exactly do we go?
Head upstairs at The Elford, on the corner of Beaufort Street and Walcott Street in Mount Lawley. Doors from 6pm, going until midnight.
