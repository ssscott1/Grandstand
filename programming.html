The method

Blocks, not guesswork

Our programming partner is Overpowered Athletics, specialists in periodised strength and conditioning. Each 6–8 week block has a clear focus — a lift to build, an engine quality to develop — so your training compounds instead of just accumulating. The full week of programming drops at the start of each week, so you can look ahead and decide exactly which classes to attend.

FAQs

Programming, answered

Who writes Grandstand's programming?
Our strength and conditioning programming is written by Overpowered Athletics in structured 6–8 week blocks, and delivered on the floor by our CrossFit Level 2 coach Jack Roberts and our team of qualified CrossFit trainers.
What is a training block?
A 6–8 week cycle with a clear strength and conditioning focus. Instead of random daily workouts, each week builds on the last — so you finish every block measurably stronger and fitter than you started it.
When is the programming released?
The full week of programming is released at the start of each week — so you can see exactly what's coming and decide which classes to attend.
What is Terrible Tuesday?
Grandstand's own weekly tradition — a classic, high-intensity CrossFit hit-out every Tuesday. One day a week the periodisation steps aside for an old-school test of grit the whole gym suffers through together, with scaled options for every level.
Can I do extra work outside classes?
Yes. Members can do accessory work before or after class in the open gym area, or on the main gym floor whenever CrossFit classes aren't running.
Do I need to start at the beginning of a block?
No — join any time. Every session is coached and scaled to where you are, and the coaches will slot you straight into the current block.
