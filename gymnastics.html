How it works

Built into every class

FAQs

Gymnastics, answered

Is gymnastics its own class at Grandstand?
Not a separate timetable slot, no — gymnastics is one of the three pillars of every CrossFit class, alongside strength and conditioning. Your coach programs pull-ups, handstands, rope climbs and other skill work right into the daily WOD.
What if I can't do a pull-up yet?
That's completely normal and exactly what scaling is for. Every gymnastics movement has a progression — banded pull-ups, ring rows, box-assisted dips — so you build the strength and skill to get your first strict rep, then your first kipping rep, then your first muscle-up.
What movements count as gymnastics in CrossFit?
Pull-ups, muscle-ups, handstand push-ups and handstand walks, rope climbs, toes-to-bar, ring dips, pistols and push-ups — any bodyweight movement that demands control, strength and skill rather than an external load.
Can I get extra gymnastics practice outside class?
Yes — the rig, rings and rope are available any time classes aren't running, as part of open gym. Ask your coach for a skill progression to work on.
