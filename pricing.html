Membership FAQs

The fine print, minus the fine print

Is there a joining fee or lock-in contract?
We keep it simple and flexible — including FIFO-friendly arrangements. Ask us about current terms when you book your free trial.
What's included in the Unlimited membership?
Every class on the timetable — CrossFit, Hyrox engine and Olympic weightlifting specialty sessions — plus weekday open gym access from 5:30am to 7:30pm.
I work FIFO. Can my membership flex around my swing?
Yes — we've supported FIFO members for a decade with flexible arrangements on our session-based memberships. Tell us your roster and we'll sort it.
Do you offer drop-ins for visitors?
Absolutely. An unlimited week is $80, and single-session drop-ins are available — email info@grandstandcrossfit.com.au to book.
