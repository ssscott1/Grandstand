Membership FAQs

The fine print, minus the fine print

Is there a joining fee or lock-in contract?
We keep it simple and flexible — including FIFO-friendly arrangements. Ask us about current terms when you book your free trial.
What's included in the Unlimited membership?
Every class on the timetable — CrossFit, the Sunday Hyrox session and Olympic weightlifting — plus open gym at 10:30am and 1:30pm every weekday.
I work FIFO. Can my membership flex around my swing?
Yes — we've supported FIFO members for a decade with flexible arrangements on our session-based memberships. Tell us your roster and we'll sort it.
Can I put my membership on hold?
Yes — memberships can be placed on hold for a minimum of 5 days and a maximum of 28 days. We just need at least 3 days' notice before the hold starts, so let us know ahead of time and we'll set it up.
How do I cancel my membership?
We'd hate to see you go — but if life takes you elsewhere, we ask for a minimum of 3 days' notice for cancellations. Get in touch and we'll sort it, no drama.
Do I need to check in to classes?
Yes — all members must check in to each class. It helps us roster the right number of coaches and keep participant numbers at a safe, well-coached level in every session.
Do you offer drop-ins for visitors?
Absolutely. A casual single-session drop-in is $30, or grab an unlimited week for $80 — email info@grandstandcrossfit.com.au to book.
