FAQs

CrossFit questions, answered

Is CrossFit safe for beginners?
Yes — when it's coached properly. At Grandstand every class is led by an experienced coach, capped in size, and every movement has a scaled option. You'll learn the foundational movements in your first sessions and progress at your own pace.
What does a CrossFit class at Grandstand look like?
Around 60 minutes: a coached warm-up, a strength or skill block, the day's workout (WOD), and a cool-down. The coach briefs every movement, sets your scaling, and keeps an eye on you the whole way through.
How often should I train CrossFit?
Most members see great results at 3 sessions a week; many build to 4–5. Our memberships come in unlimited, 3x and 2x per week options so you can match training to your life.
Do I need to be fit before I start CrossFit?
No — that's what the classes are for. CrossFit is infinitely scalable: the same workout challenges a first-timer and a Games athlete, just at different loads and speeds.
