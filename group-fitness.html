FAQs

Group fitness, answered

What group fitness classes does Grandstand run?
Three class types, one membership: CrossFit (eight classes every weekday plus Saturday mornings), Olympic weightlifting (Wednesday 6pm and Saturday 9:30am) and a Hyrox session on Sunday at 9am. Every class is coach-led and scaled to your level.
Can I bring my kids while I train?
Yes — this is one of our favourite things about Grandstand. We have a gated kids creche area with a child-proof lock, right inside the gym. Kids can relax on the bean bags, play with the toys, draw on the chalkboard or watch Netflix while you train.
Do I need experience to join a group fitness class?
No. Every class is coached and every movement is scaled, so complete beginners train alongside experienced athletes — and your first class is a free trial.
Which class should I start with?
Most people start with a CrossFit class — strength, conditioning and skills in one coached hour. From there, add weightlifting or the Sunday Hyrox session. Book a free trial and we'll point you at the right one.
