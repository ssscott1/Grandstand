Our story

Since 2014

Grandstand CrossFit was founded by coaches Matt Evans and Justin Samulski, who wanted to build the gym they'd want to train in: elite coaching standards, zero ego, and a community that makes you feel like part of the tribe from the moment you walk in the door.

The name comes from our home turf — a stone's throw from the grandstands of HBF Park on Parry Street. A decade on, thousands of Perth locals have squatted, sweated and celebrated PBs under our roof.

Our promise is on the wall: coaching with a side of humour, good looks, compassion and patience — and workouts tailored to suit you.
