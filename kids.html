FAQs

Parents ask, we answer

How do the 6-week training blocks work?
Kids training runs in structured 6-week blocks, with each week building on the last — movement skills, strength basics and confidence, wrapped in games. Register your interest below and we'll confirm start dates, times and age groups for the next block.
What ages are kids classes for?
We group sessions by age and stage so every kid trains with peers. Get in touch and we'll let you know the current groups and times.
Is it safe for kids to do CrossFit-style training?
Yes — our kids sessions are built on bodyweight movement, coordination and games. It's about moving well and having fun, not loading up barbells. Coaches are experienced with young athletes.
Can parents train at the same time?
That's the dream setup — and we'll help you find class times that line up so the whole family trains under one roof.
