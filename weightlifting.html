FAQs

Weightlifting questions, answered

Do I need weightlifting experience to join?
No. Our weightlifting classes focus on approach and technique from the first session. You'll start with a PVC pipe or empty bar and earn load as your positions improve.
What's the difference between CrossFit and Olympic weightlifting classes?
CrossFit classes mix strength, conditioning and gymnastics. Weightlifting classes are dedicated entirely to the snatch and clean & jerk — more sets, more technical coaching, more barbell.
Will weightlifting make me better at CrossFit and Hyrox?
Massively. Better barbell technique means more efficient WODs, and raw strength is the foundation under every sled push and wall ball. It's the highest-leverage hour in your training week.
