FAQs

Group training, answered

How is group training different from CrossFit classes?
Group training sessions use the same functional movements and coaching standards, with less barbell complexity — think dumbbells, kettlebells, machines and bodyweight. It's a great entry point, and plenty of members do both.
I'm unfit and a bit intimidated. Is this for me?
That feeling is exactly why we coach the way we do. Every session scales to your level, nobody is left behind, and the crew you sweat with becomes the reason you keep showing up. Start with a free trial and see.
How many people are in a class?
Small enough that the coach knows your name, your niggles and your goals — and can actually coach you, not just count you.
